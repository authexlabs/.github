# Authex Labs

Keep your company email working.

Authex Labs is developing a managed company email service for small and medium-sized businesses. Launch is in progress.

The focus is sending and access: diagnosing problems, making supported fixes with the customer’s authorization, and checking the results.

Authex Labs is owned by [Hemanth Vishnu Advisory](https://hemanthvishnu.com), the sole proprietorship of Hemanth Vishnu Akula, based in New Delhi, India.
